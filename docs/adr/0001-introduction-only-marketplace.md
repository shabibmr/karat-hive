# Introduction-only marketplace (no on-platform settlement)

Karat Hive brokers introductions between Customers and Vendors. Payment, escrow, delivery, and proof of a completed sale are out of scope: after Acceptance and Identity Reveal, parties settle off-platform. The platform’s authoritative outcome is a Connection (and optional Reviews), not a closed transaction.

**Why.** Building payments and escrow would add financial licensing, liability for funds and goods, and a different product shape. The chosen model keeps the platform light and matches the PO decision that parties deal at their own risk. Analytics measure introductions (Connections, Talk usage), not sale volume.

**Considered options.** Full checkout with escrow; platform-mediated payment without escrow. Rejected for v1 regulatory and scope cost.
