# Request-driven marketplace, not vendor inventory

Demand starts with the Customer: they publish a Request describing what they want to buy or sell. Vendors do not list a catalogue of stock for browsing. Eligible Vendors are matched to Requests and respond with Offers.

**Why.** Gold jewellery demand is high-intent and often one-off; a request feed turns Vendor effort into competing responses rather than perpetual inventory management. Identity Masking and Offer comparison only make sense in a reverse-demand loop. Catalogue e-commerce would invert the product and dilute the core value loop.

**Considered options.** Vendor catalogue with cart/checkout; hybrid browse-and-request. Rejected for v1 to keep the loop simple and mask-friendly.
