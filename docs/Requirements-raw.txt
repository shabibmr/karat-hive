Karat Hive is a digital jewellery marketplace where:

1. Customers create Requests for buying or selling gold and related products.
2. Verified Vendors respond with Offers.
3. Customers evaluate and select a Vendor.


Three User Types
1. Customer : User who creates Requests to buy or sell items
2. Vendor/Jeweler  : Verified business/Jeweler responding to Requests
3. Platform Admins : Controls the platform. Administrators.
